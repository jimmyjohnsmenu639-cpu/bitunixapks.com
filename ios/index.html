Bitunix App for iPhone & iPad

Quick heads-up: there's no such thing as a Bitunix APK for iPhone — APK files are Android-only. On iOS you download from the Apple App Store. Here's exactly how.

Install in 2 minutes

Download from the App Store

  1. Open the App Store on your iPhone or iPad and search "Bitunix".
  2. Verify it's official — check the developer name is Bitunix and the icon matches the official branding. Safest: tap the iOS button on bitunix.com/download, which deep-links to the real listing.
  3. Tap Get and confirm with Face ID / Touch ID. The app is free.
  4. Open and sign up / log in — your account works across iPhone, Android and web.
  5. Enable Face ID login + 2FA in the app's security settings before depositing anything.

Requirements: iOS 13 or later, ~300 MB free space. The app supports English, Urdu-region locales and 12+ other languages.

Troubleshooting

App not in your country's App Store?

Bitunix's App Store availability varies by region. If search shows nothing, you have two fixes:

Option A — Change App Store region

  • Go to Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases → View Account → Country/Region
  • Switch to a supported region (e.g. where Bitunix is listed), accept the terms, then search again
  • Note: you'll need a payment method valid for that region for paid apps — Bitunix itself is free, so this rarely blocks you

Option B — Use the web app (easiest)

  • Open Safari → go to bitunix.com → log in
  • Tap Share → Add to Home Screen — you get an app-like icon with full trading, no App Store needed

Never install Bitunix via third-party "iOS installer" sites or enterprise certificates — those are a classic way to get a fake app that steals your login.
