Bitunix restricted countries

Bitunix is not available everywhere. Before you sign up or deposit, check whether your country is on the restricted list, because accounts from restricted regions can be closed.

The official list

Where Bitunix is not available

Bitunix publishes a Restricted Regions and User Eligibility Notice that lists every country and region where its services are not offered. The notice was last updated on 5 August 2026, and the list can change at any time. Always check the official notice before you sign up, because this page is a summary and the official notice is the final word.

If your country is on the list below, do not try to sign up anyway. The sections further down explain why, and what to do instead.

Important warning

Do not use a VPN to get around the list

Accessing Bitunix through a VPN, proxy or GPS spoofing to hide your location is not permitted. Bitunix may suspend or terminate the account, close open positions, or restrict access to the platform.

A VPN does not make a restricted account safe. It puts your funds at risk, because an account closed for breaking the rules is much harder to recover than an account that was never opened.

If you are restricted

What to do instead

Use a trading platform that is licensed to operate in your own country, so your account, deposits and withdrawals are fully supported. If you prefer not to use a centralised exchange at all, a decentralised exchange lets you trade from your own wallet without an account.

Either way, check the platform's own availability rules before you deposit anything, so you do not run into the same problem a second time.

One more restriction

Card purchases have a wider list

Credit and debit card purchases on Bitunix have a wider restricted list than the main service. A country that can use the exchange may still be blocked from buying crypto by card. Check the official notice for the card purchase list before you plan a deposit by card.
