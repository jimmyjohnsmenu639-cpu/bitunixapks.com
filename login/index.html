Bitunix login

Logging in to Bitunix takes less than a minute when everything works. This guide walks you through the sign in steps for the app and the website, then fixes the problems that most often lock people out.

Sign in

How to log in, step by step

The same account works on the app and the website.

  1. Open the official Bitunix app on your phone, or go to bitunix.com in your browser. Type the address yourself, and never log in from a link in an email or message you did not expect.
  2. Tap or click Log In at the top of the screen.
  3. Enter your registered email address or phone number, then your password. Use the same details you signed up with.
  4. Enter your 2FA code if enabled. Open your authenticator app and type in the 6-digit code shown for Bitunix.
  5. Reach your dashboard. Your balances, markets and open positions are all there, and they sync between the app and the website.

One account, every device: there is no separate login for the app and the website. If you registered with an email address, log in with that email address everywhere. If you registered with a phone number, use that number everywhere.

Troubleshooting

Login problems, fixed

Work through these in order. Most lockouts are solved by the first fix that matches your problem.

On the login page, tap Forgot Password and enter the email address or phone number on your account. Bitunix sends a reset link or code to your registered email. Follow it to set a new password, then log in again. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and confirm you typed the address you registered with.

If you saved a backup key when you set up 2FA, use it to restore your authenticator on a new phone. If you did not save one, you will need to verify your identity through official Bitunix support before 2FA can be reset. This takes time by design, because it protects your funds. Once you are back in, store your new backup key somewhere safe and offline.

Several failed login attempts in a row can temporarily lock your account. Wait a while, then try again with the correct details. Do not keep guessing, because more failed attempts can extend the lock. If the lock does not lift, contact official Bitunix support from the official site or app and be ready to prove the account is yours.

For email codes, check your spam or promotions folder first. For authenticator codes, make sure your phone clock is set to automatic, because a wrong time makes codes fail. Then ask for a new code and enter it quickly, since codes expire within seconds.

Update your browser to the latest version, clear cache and cookies for the site, and try again. If it still fails, try another device or network to rule out a problem on your side. If the official site itself is down, wait and retry later.

Stay safe

Login security checklist

Most stolen accounts are lost to fake login pages, not to hacking. Run through this list once and you close the common gaps.

  • Only ever log in through bitunix.com. Check the domain spelling carefully and look for HTTPS before typing your password.
  • Never share your password or one-time codes with anyone, not even someone claiming to be support.
  • Real Bitunix support never asks for your password, authenticator codes or backup key. Anyone who does is a scammer.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app for stronger protection than SMS alone.
  • Review your active sessions in account settings now and then, and sign out any device you do not recognise.
  • Avoid logging in on public Wi-Fi. If you must log in away from home, use your mobile data instead.
